Privacy Policy — Pinbolita

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Pinbolita does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no user accounts, and it does not send information anywhere. Everything it saves stays on your device.

This policy applies to Pinbolita, published by Iván Rodríguez:

Android — io.github.ivan_i1.pinbolita

iOS — io.github.ivan-i1.pinbolita

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for, collect or receive any personal information. Pinbolita does not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, IP address or usage analytics.

There are no user accounts and no sign-in. We operate no servers, and no information from the app ever reaches us or any third party.

Information stored on your device

Pinbolita saves a small amount of data locally so your game survives being closed and reopened. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

  • Your settings — sound and vibration toggles, volumes and physics preferences.
  • Your table layout — the arrangement of elements you have placed on the table.
  • A reference to any custom sound you choose — see the next section.

Custom sounds you choose

Pinbolita lets you replace the built-in sound effects with an audio file of your own. If you use this feature, your device's standard file picker opens and you select a file. The app then copies that single file into its own private storage area so it can play it back.

That copy is never uploaded, shared or transmitted. The app does not browse, index or read any other file on your device — only the one file you explicitly pick each time. Choosing a new sound replaces the previous copy, and clearing the custom sound deletes it.

Permissions

Pinbolita requests only what it needs to function: vibration for haptic feedback, and access to the single file you select in the picker.

It does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts or activity recognition permissions. The app plays audio but has no recording capability of any kind.

Children's privacy

Pinbolita is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request the deletion of.

Third parties

Pinbolita contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software development kits.

The app is distributed through app stores — such as Google Play — which operate independently of us and may collect information about downloads, installs and crashes under their own privacy policies. We never receive personal information from them. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles that data.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR give you rights over personal data held about you, including rights of access, correction and erasure. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. You can remove everything the app has stored by uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If Pinbolita ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect. Since the app does not communicate with us, we cannot notify you directly — please check this page if you want to stay informed.
