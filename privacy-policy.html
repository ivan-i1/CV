Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

These applications do not collect, store or transmit any personal data. They contain no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no user accounts, and they do not send information anywhere. Everything they save stays on your device.

This policy covers the following applications (“the Apps”), published by Iván Rodríguez:

Bounce Fidget — io.github.ivan_i1.bounce_fidget

Pinbolita — io.github.ivan_i1.pinbolita

Information we collect

None. We do not ask for, collect or receive any personal information. Specifically, the Apps do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, IP address or usage analytics.

There are no user accounts and no sign-in. We have no servers, and no information from the Apps ever reaches us or any third party.

Information stored on your device

The Apps save a small amount of data locally so your preferences survive being closed and reopened. This data never leaves your device, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

  • A reference to any custom sound you choose (both Apps) — see the next section.
  • Your settings (Pinbolita) — such as sound and vibration toggles, volumes and physics preferences.
  • Your table layout (Pinbolita) — the arrangement of elements you have placed.

Bounce Fidget stores nothing but the custom sound references; its settings reset each time the App is restarted.

Custom sounds you choose

Both Apps let you replace the built-in sound effects with an audio file of your own. If you use this feature, your device's standard file picker opens and you select a file. The App then copies that single file into its own private storage area so it can play it.

That copy is never uploaded, shared or transmitted. The Apps do not browse, index or read any other file on your device — only the one file you explicitly pick each time. Choosing a new sound replaces the previous copy, and clearing the custom sound deletes it.

Permissions

The Apps request only what they need to function: vibration for haptic feedback, access to the single file you select in the picker, and (in Bounce Fidget) the motion sensor used to tilt the ball. Motion sensor readings are used in the moment to move the ball on screen and are never recorded or stored.

Children's privacy

The Apps are suitable for all ages. Because they collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children either. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request the deletion of.

Third parties

The Apps contain no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software development kits.

The Apps are distributed through app stores — such as Google Play — which operate independently of us and may collect information about downloads, installs and crashes under their own privacy policies. We never receive personal information from them. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles that data.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR give you rights over personal data held about you, including rights of access, correction and erasure. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete. You can remove everything the Apps have stored by uninstalling them.

Changes to this policy

If an App ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect. Since the Apps do not communicate with us, we cannot notify you directly — please check this page if you want to stay informed.
